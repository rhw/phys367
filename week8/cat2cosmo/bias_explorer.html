One lens bin and one source bin, real-space 3×2pt with a Gaussian covariance. The data contain a systematic that the analysis ignores. It fits only Ωm, S8 and the galaxy bias b. Setup: fsky = 0.1, nsrc = 5 arcmin⁻², nlens = 0.2 arcmin⁻², σe = 0.26; 20 log θ bins in 2.5′–250′ per probe. CCL halofit + Eisenstein–Hu; Ωb, h, ns fixed; weak Gaussian priors σ(Ωm, S8, b) = (0.3, 0.5, 1).

Data shifts come from full CCL runs, precomputed on grids in Δzs, Δzl, ηIA and kd. The response is exactly linear in the baryon depth A, quadratic in AIA, and multiplicative in m. Shifts from different systematics are added. The parameter response uses the derivkit DALI doublet (J and H at the fiducial), which matches a full nonlinear CCL refit to better than 1% in the notebook's tests. “Shift/σ” is (best fit − truth) / marginal σ; flat priors Ωm ∈ [0.1, 0.6], S8 ∈ [0.5, 1.1], b ∈ [0.5, 3.5].
